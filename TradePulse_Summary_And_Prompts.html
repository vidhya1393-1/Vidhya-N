PROJECT EXPORT GUIDE

TradePulse - Android Investment & Market Tracker

Complete project structure, prompt summary, and download instructions for Google AI Studio.

📥 How to Download Your Project in AI Studio

  1. Download Entire Project as ZIP:
    Look at the top-right corner of the AI Studio window. Click on the Download / Export (or Settings ⋮) icon and select "Download ZIP" or "Export Project as ZIP". This packages all source code, resources, and Gradle files to your computer.
  2. Download the APK directly:
    Under the same Settings / Export menu, choose "Build APK" to download the compiled `.apk` file that installs directly onto your Android device.
  3. Download this HTML file directly:
    In the File Explorer on the left sidebar, click on TradePulse_Summary_And_Prompts.html, right-click, and select Download. You can then open it in any web browser.
  4. Push to GitHub:
    Click "Connect to GitHub" or the GitHub icon at the top to publish the entire repository directly to your personal GitHub account.

📱 Summary of Features Implemented

1. Markets Dashboard

Live session ticker, benchmark indices carousel/list (NIFTY, SENSEX, NASDAQ), Fear & Greed sentiment gauge, and sector heatmap.

2. Live Stocks & Technicals

Stock symbol search with instant results, 52W range bars, valuation ratios (P/E, P/B), and simulated paper trading.

3. Candlestick & Volume Chart

Interactive OHLCV wicks, volume bars, touch crosshair inspection, and toggleable EMA 20, Bollinger Bands, and VWAP overlays.

4. Institutional Indicators

RSI (14), MACD (12, 26, 9) with histogram crossover, Moving Averages (EMA 20, 50/200 DMA Golden Cross), and Pivot Levels (S1/S2/R1/R2).

5. Derivatives & F&O Suite

Full Option Chain table (Calls, Puts, ATM Strike Highlight, OI, IV), Futures basis/premium, Put-Call Ratio (PCR) meter, and Max Pain.

6. Real-Time Price Alerts

Set trigger thresholds (Crosses Above / Drops Below) with top floating notification banners and a dedicated Alerts Center.

7. Multi-Asset Portfolio

Net worth tracking for Equities, Mutual Funds, IPOs, and Gold/Bonds with live P&L and asset allocation distribution.

8. IPOs, Mutual Funds & SIP

Ongoing and upcoming IPOs with Grey Market Premium (GMP), mutual fund schemes, and interactive SIP wealth compounding calculator.

🛠️ Technical Stack & Architecture

  • Language: Kotlin 100%
  • UI Framework: Jetpack Compose + Material Design 3 (M3)
  • Local Persistence: Android Room Database (`AppDatabase`, `HoldingDao`, `TransactionDao`, `WatchlistDao`, `AlertDao`)
  • Architecture: MVVM (Model-View-ViewModel) + StateFlow & Coroutines
  • Design & Assets: Custom fintech bull adaptive app icon across all mipmap densities
